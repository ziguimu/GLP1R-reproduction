# WSL alignment archive decision / WSL 对齐文件归档说明

## English

A supplemental WSL inventory found 49 ColabFold alignment/search-output files (10,284,071 bytes). Two files are byte-identical to files already represented in the `archive-2026-10-31` release. The other 47 have 34 distinct SHA-256 values and contain database-derived multiple-sequence alignments or related search outputs. Their underlying sequence-database redistribution terms have not been established from the local records, so these files are retained locally and excluded from the public release pending a rights/source review. This is an explicit scope limitation, not a claim that the data are unrecoverable or unimportant. The local file-level inventory records their source paths, sizes, hashes, and disposition.

## 中文

补充 WSL 清单发现 49 个 ColabFold 比对/搜索输出文件，共 10,284,071 字节。其中 2 个与 `archive-2026-10-31` 发布版已收录文件逐字节相同。其余 47 个包含数据库派生的多序列比对或相关搜索输出，共有 34 个不同 SHA-256 值。本地记录尚不足以确认这些序列数据库内容的再分发条款，因此暂时保留在本地，不纳入公开 Release，等待核实来源和许可。这是明确记录的范围限制，不表示这些文件不可恢复或没有研究价值。本地逐文件清单保留其来源路径、大小、哈希及处置状态。
